Start here

Before you test

  1. Start everything with pnpm dev. After an update, stop it and start it again.
  2. Open the player site. It opens in Georgian; the language button in the header switches to English or Russian.
  3. Open the backoffice and sign in as admin@metrobet.local. The password is in the file apps/api/.env, on the line SEED_ADMIN_PASSWORD. The admin can see and do everything.

Verification codes. The site never sends real emails or SMS. Open localhost:4000/api/dev/outbox?to=YOUR_EMAIL to see the last code sent to that address (sign-up and password reset).

Player site

Your account

Sign up and sign in

  • Sign up (top right): email or mobile number, a password and your date of birth. You must be 25 or older.
  • Confirm the account with the 6-digit code from the code inbox. Until then the header shows a small orange dot.
  • Forgot password works the same way: the reset code is in the code inbox.

The account menu

Click your email in the header: Wallet, My bets, Account settings, Responsible gaming, Log out.

Player site

Money

Wallet

Where: click the balance in the header

  • The header shows what you can bet with: your cash plus any bonus money. A gift icon means bonus money is part of it.
  • The wallet page shows your cash (what you can withdraw), any bonus money, the bonus progress, pending withdrawals and every transaction.
  • Free test money: the dashed "development only" box adds ₾10–₾500 instantly. It does not trigger the welcome bonus.

Deposit through the bank

Where: Wallet → Deposit

  1. Choose TBC Bank or Bank of Georgia and an amount, then press Continue to the bank.
  2. A test bank page opens. Enter one of the cards below, any future expiry (for example 12/30) and any 3-digit CVC.
  3. You come back to the wallet with a message saying whether the money arrived.

You can deposit up to ₾1,000 in total without verifying your identity. Past that, the deposit asks you to verify first.

Withdraw

Where: Wallet → Withdraw

  • You need a confirmed account, a verified identity and a card you have deposited with. The page tells you which step is missing.
  • The amount leaves your balance at once and waits for staff approval. You can cancel it while it is pending (it shows on the wallet page).
  • Staff approve or reject it in the backoffice. If they reject it, the money comes back and you see their reason.

Bonus money

  • Your first bank deposit of ₾20 or more gets a 100% bonus, up to ₾500.
  • Bets use your cash first, then bonus money. Winnings from bonus money come back as bonus money.
  • Wager the bonus 10 times within 14 days and it turns into cash you can withdraw. Every casino bet counts; sports tickets count at odds of 1.50 or more.
  • The Bonus card on the wallet page shows the progress, for example "₾340 of ₾1,000 wagered".
  • Withdrawing before that cancels the bonus. The withdraw page asks you to tick a box to confirm.
Player site

Identity check

Where: My account → Identity → Verify my identity

  1. A test verification page opens. Enter a first and last name, an 11-digit personal number, a date of birth and a document number.
  2. The date of birth must be the same as on your account, or the check fails.
  3. You come back to the Identity tab with the result.
Personal number ends inResult
0000Rejected document could not be verified

One personal number can belong to only one account. Using it on a second account is rejected.

Player site

Responsible gaming

Where: My account → Responsible gaming

ToolHow it works
LimitsDeposit, loss and stake limits, each per 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. Lowering a limit works at once; raising or removing one waits 24 hours. A bet or deposit over the limit is blocked with a message saying how much is left.
Reality checkA reminder every 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes: how long you have played, what you staked and won, with Keep playing or Log out.
Take a break24 hours, 7 or 30 days. Needs your password. You are signed out and cannot sign in until it ends.
Self-exclusion6 months, 1 year or permanently. Same as a break, for longer. Cannot be undone early.

Use a separate test account for breaks and self-exclusion: you will be locked out of it.

Player site

Sports betting

  • Sports, Live and the home page list football, basketball, tennis and eSoccer. Odds change live. eSoccer matches last about 9 minutes: the quickest way to see a bet settle.
  • Filter by day and league; the ★ saves a league as a favourite.
  • Open a match for every market. Live football and eSoccer matches show a match tracker: timeline, stats, goals and cards.
  • Betslip: click odds to add them, choose Single or Accumulator, set a stake (ALL uses your whole balance) and place the bet.
  • My bets: Open and Settled tickets. Cash out an open ticket early. When a ticket wins, a notification pops up and the balance updates by itself.
Player site

Casino & crash games

  • Slots: categories, providers, search, favourites and recently played. Real money: the Golden Fleece slots and Supra Fortune (Metrobet's own slot). Other slots are demo only.
  • Crash: Metro Jet and Longshot, Metrobet's own crash games. Bet before take-off, cash out before the plane flies away.
  • Every game can be played in Demo (free play money) or for real money from your balance.
Player site

Promotions

  • Banners on the home and section pages, and the Promotions page, open each promotion's page.
  • Wheel of fortune: press Join, then play slots. Every ₾50 bet earns one spin; every spin wins free spins.
  • Welcome bonus: the first-deposit offer (see Bonus money), with a Deposit now button.
Staff tool

Backoffice

Sign in at localhost:3001. Every change staff make asks for a reason and is saved in the audit log.

ScreenWhat you can do
DashboardToday's numbers: profit per product (casino and sports), deposits, bonus cost, new and active players, open tickets, money owed to players.
PromotionsCreate, edit, drag to reorder, switch on or off and delete promotions. The page builder has text, steps, countdown, prizes, FAQ, terms, a Join button, a prize wheel and a deposit bonus. A live preview shows the result; changes reach the site immediately.
PlayersSearch by email, phone or ID. A player's page shows balance, details and limits, plus tabs: Transactions, Payments, Bonuses, Identity, Sports tickets, Game rounds. Suspend or reactivate the account; cancel an active bonus.
PaymentsWithdrawal queue: Approve sends the money to the card, Reject returns it (the player sees your reason). "All payments" lists every deposit and withdrawal.
KYCIdentity checks waiting for a person. You see the document details next to the date of birth the player registered with. Approve or reject with a reason.
BetsEvery sports ticket. Open one to void it (the stake is refunded) or settle a selection by hand.
SportsbookLive and upcoming events with stakes and the worst-case loss. Suspend an event or a single market (players cannot bet until you resume). Rules: margin and stake/payout limits per sport or league. An event page shows the loss on each outcome.
Audit logEvery staff action: who, what, before and after, and why.
